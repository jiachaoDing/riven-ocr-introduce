Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Riven Lens

What is Riven Lens and how does it work? ▼

Riven Lens is a free Chrome extension that uses OCR (Optical Character Recognition) technology to automatically read your Warframe Riven mod stats from screenshots. It then auto-fills the warframe.market listing form, saving you from manually typing every attribute.

Is Riven Lens safe to use? Will I get banned? ▼

Yes, Riven Lens is completely safe. It only interacts with the warframe.market website (not the actual game) and performs the same actions you would do manually — just much faster. It doesn't modify game files or use any exploits. The extension is open source, so you can verify this yourself.

Does it support all Warframe riven mods? ▼

Yes! Riven Lens recognizes all weapon types including Rifles, Shotguns, Pistols, Melee, Archguns, and Kitguns. Our OCR model is trained on thousands of riven images to ensure accurate recognition across all weapons and stat combinations.

Is Riven Lens free? ▼

Yes, Riven Lens is 100% free to use. No subscriptions, no hidden fees. We're fellow Tenno who wanted to make trading rivens less tedious. If you find it helpful, consider leaving a review on the Chrome Web Store!
