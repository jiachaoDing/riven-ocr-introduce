Privacy Policy for Riven OCR Assistant

Last Updated: January 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Riven OCR Assistant ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our browser extension.

2. Data Collection and Image Processing

The core function of this Extension is to recognize text from images (specifically "Riven Mod" screenshots from the game Warframe).

  • Image Data: When you use the OCR feature, the selected image or screenshot is sent to a remote server (the OCR Backend) for processing. This data is used exclusively for optical character recognition (OCR) to extract text attributes.
  • No Storage: We do not store your image data permanently. Images are processed in-memory and discarded immediately after the text extraction is complete.

3. Extension Permissions

The Extension requires specific permissions to function correctly:

  • storage: This permission is necessary to save your personalized settings (e.g., custom OCR URL, UI theme preferences) and local recognition history on your device. This data stays within your browser.
  • host_permissions (warframe.market): Used to automatically fill recognized data into the auction forms on the warframe.market website.
  • host_permissions (OCR API): Used to communicate with the remote OCR server for image processing.

4. No Tracking and No Personal Information

We believe in total privacy. Riven OCR Assistant:

  • Does not collect personal identification information (names, emails, etc.).
  • Does not track your browsing history or search queries.
  • Does not share any data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption, for all data transmitted between the Extension and the OCR server to ensure your data remains secure during transit.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository or official communication channels.
